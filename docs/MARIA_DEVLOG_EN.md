# MARIA DEVLOG

> This document is an author-driven development log describing the evolution of Maria from a simple assistant concept into a local AI character ecosystem.

## Beginning

Maria originally started as a personal AI assistant idea.
Over time, the project gradually shifted away from pure utility and toward presence, memory, subjective perception and authored identity.

The project evolved through experimentation, failures, rewrites, regressions and architectural changes.

This document serves as a historical archive of that process.
